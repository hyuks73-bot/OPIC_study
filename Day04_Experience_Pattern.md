# 📅 Day 4: 과거 경험 패턴 (Past Experience Pattern)

> **목표**: OPIc 시험에서 자주 등장하는 **"과거 경험 묘사"** 질문에 자연스럽게 답변하기
> **핵심**: 시간 표현 + 상황 설명 + 전개 + 결과/감정 + 마무리 소감

---

## 1. 과거 경험 질문 유형

OPIc 시험에서 **Combo Question**의 3번째 질문으로 거의 항상 나오는 패턴입니다.

> **묘사 → 루틴 → 과거 경험** (Day 2 → Day 3 → Day 4)

| 질문 유형 | 예시 |
|:---|:---|
| 특별한 경험 | "Tell me about a **memorable experience** you had at the park." |
| 처음 해본 경험 | "Tell me about **the first time** you went jogging." |
| 최근 경험 | "Tell me about **the last time** you listened to music." |
| 인상 깊었던 경험 | "Tell me about **an experience** related to music that you still remember." |

---

## 2. 과거 경험 답변 5단계 공식 (IH/AL 틀)

```
1️⃣ [Intro: 경험 소개]     → "I remember one time when..."
2️⃣ [Background: 배경 설명] → "It was [시간/날씨/상황]..."
3️⃣ [Event: 사건 전개]     → "What happened was..."
4️⃣ [Result: 결과/감정]    → "In the end, I felt..."
5️⃣ [Outro: 소감/교훈]     → "That experience made me realize..."
```

---

## 3. 핵심 시간 표현 & 연결어

| 표현 | 의미 | 예시 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `I remember one time when...` | ~했던 기억이 나요 | I remember one time when I was jogging... |
| `It was about + 시간 + ago` | ~전쯤이었어요 | It was about six months ago. |
| `At some point...` | 어느 순간에 | At some point, I took a wrong turn. |
| `Before I knew it...` | 어느새 | Before I knew it, I had run 15km. |
| `In the end...` | 결국에는 | In the end, I had no choice but to walk. |
| `That experience made me realize...` | 그 경험이 깨닫게 해줬어요 | That experience gave me real confidence. |
| `I still remember how...` | 아직도 ~했던 게 기억나요 | I still remember how exhausted I was. |

---

## 4. 🎯 맞춤 실전 답변 1: 우연히 15km를 달린 날

### ❓ 콤보 질문 3번 (Combo Q3)
> *"You mentioned that you enjoy jogging at a park. Can you tell me about a memorable experience you had at the park?"*
> (공원에서 조깅하는 것을 좋아한다고 하셨는데, 공원에서 특별히 기억에 남는 경험을 말씀해 주시겠어요?)

---

### 🗣️ IH/AL 맞춤 스크립트

**Sentence 1**
> I remember one particularly **memorable experience** at the waterfront park near my house.

**Sentence 2**
> It was about **six months ago on a Sunday morning** — I headed out for my usual weekend jog along the waterfront trail.

**Sentence 3**
> At some point, I discovered a **newly extended path** that I had never seen before, so I decided to explore it and ran for about an extra 15 minutes.

**Sentence 4**
> But on the way back, I somehow **took a wrong turn** and ended up on an unfamiliar path — I was completely lost.

**Sentence 5**
> I kept **running around trying to find my way back**, and before I knew it, I had run a total of **15 kilometers**.

**Sentence 6**
> My legs **completely gave out** after that, and I had no choice but to walk the rest of the way home.

**Sentence 7**
> It was the **first time in my life** that I had ever run 15 kilometers — even if it was by accident.

**Sentence 8**
> But strangely, that experience gave me **real confidence** — I thought, if I can run 15km by accident, then with a little more training, I could actually **complete a half marathon** someday.

---

### 📝 전체 스크립트 (연결해서 읽기)

> I remember one particularly memorable experience at the waterfront park near my house. It was about six months ago on a Sunday morning — I headed out for my usual weekend jog along the waterfront trail. At some point, I discovered a newly extended path that I had never seen before, so I decided to explore it and ran for about an extra 15 minutes. But on the way back, I somehow took a wrong turn and ended up on an unfamiliar path — I was completely lost. I kept running around trying to find my way back, and before I knew it, I had run a total of 15 kilometers. My legs completely gave out after that, and I had no choice but to walk the rest of the way home. It was the first time in my life that I had ever run 15 kilometers — even if it was by accident. But strangely, that experience gave me real confidence — I thought, if I can run 15km by accident, then with a little more training, I could actually complete a half marathon someday.

## 5. 🎯 맞춤 실전 답변 2: 윤도현 락밴드 콘서트

### ❓ 콤보 질문 3번 (Combo Q3)
> *"You said you enjoy listening to music. Can you tell me about a memorable experience related to music?"*
> (음악 듣기를 좋아한다고 하셨는데, 음악과 관련하여 가장 기억에 남는 경험을 말씀해 주시겠어요?)

---

### 🗣️ IH/AL 맞춤 스크립트

**Sentence 1**
> Actually, I have a really **memorable experience** related to music — it was a rock concert I attended **before I got married**.

**Sentence 2**
> My wife — who was my girlfriend at the time — and I went to see **Yoon Do-hyun Band**, one of Korea's most legendary rock bands, **live in concert**.

**Sentence 3**
> We had **standing seats right next to the speakers**, so the music was absolutely **thunderously loud** — you could feel it in your chest.

**Sentence 4**
> The whole crowd was **singing along passionately** to every single song, and the energy in the venue was just electric.

**Sentence 5**
> At that moment, it felt like **everyone in the venue — thousands of complete strangers — had all become one**.

**Sentence 6**
> It was such a **special and unforgettable memory**, not just because of the incredible music, but because I got to share that moment with the person who later became my wife.

---

### 📝 전체 스크립트 (연결해서 읽기)

> Actually, I have a really memorable experience related to music — it was a rock concert I attended before I got married. My wife — who was my girlfriend at the time — and I went to see Yoon Do-hyun Band, one of Korea's most legendary rock bands, live in concert. We had standing seats right next to the speakers, so the music was absolutely thunderously loud — you could feel it in your chest. The whole crowd was singing along passionately to every single song, and the energy in the venue was just electric. At that moment, it felt like everyone in the venue — thousands of complete strangers — had all become one. It was such a special and unforgettable memory, not just because of the incredible music, but because I got to share that moment with the person who later became my wife.

## 7. 📝 Day 4 실습 과제 (Action Item)

1. 위 두 스크립트를 **시간 순서 표현**(`At some point`, `Before I knew it`, `In the end`)에 유의하며 **3번 낭독**하기
2. 각 스크립트의 **AL 업그레이드 표현**에 밑줄을 그으며 소리 내어 읽기
3. 오늘 배운 **핵심 표현 5개** 암기 후 직접 문장 만들어 보기
